# Customer Success Managers V3 design read

## Existing identity to preserve

- **Concept:** customer journeys held in a healthy orbit around clear ownership.
- **Palette:** deep teal, aqua, coral accent, pale mint canvas, dark green ink.
- **Voice:** operational, specific, calm, and accountable. The site should describe preparation, coordination, evidence, and escalation without pretending remote staff own commercial or product decisions.
- **Primary visual asset:** the existing customer-orbit hero image and subsidiary mark.

## V3 direction

The redesign keeps the orbit identity but removes generic template signals. Large editorial headings, asymmetric orbit cards, concise status labels, and visible workflow boundaries create a distinct customer-success operations system. The shell stays compact and sticky. Mobile navigation is a deliberate disclosure rather than a horizontally scrolling row. Content pages use topic imagery, a heading-derived contents rail, and restrained mint surfaces for long-form readability.

## Conversion composition

The contact first fold uses a balanced two-column composition. Parent-company review evidence is on the left and the complete inquiry form is on the right. The official Stealth Agents team section follows immediately. Scheduling appears only after a validated inquiry, on `/thank-you`.

## Guardrails

- Do not borrow another subsidiary's copy, palette, illustrations, or service language.
- Do not fabricate outcomes, guarantees, awards, testimonials, or company facts.
- Keep commercial, pricing, renewal, product, privacy, security, and relationship decisions with authorized customer teams.
- Avoid decorative numbering and visible long dashes.
- Every visual flourish must remain readable at 360, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixels.
